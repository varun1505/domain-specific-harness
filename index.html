How to build one
Five things to tune. Same loop.
A harness I built
Let me show you one I built.
Jyotish is Indian astrology. You give a birth date, time and place.
It works out where the planets were at that moment. Then it reads meaning from that chart.
Why I picked it: strict rules, exact maths, and nothing to tell you when you’re wrong.
This is not about whether astrology works. It’s about whether AI can follow the rules.
Why this is hard
Easy to get wrong. Hard to notice.
Jyotish splits your life into periods, each linked to a planet.
Get one small rule wrong, and a date moves by ten years.
The answer still reads perfectly.
No error. No warning. Just a wrong date.
Let’s follow one question · a made-up example
Rahul, 32, Pune
“When will I become a millionaire?”
Let’s see what each of the five does with it.
1 · Context · a real run of the harness
Code works out his chart. AI reads it.

Code works out

  • Born 2 Oct 1994, 9:30 pm, Pune: Taurus rising
  • Mercury rules his savings house. Jupiter rules his gains house.
  • Today he is in the Moon’s life period, until 2031

The AI gets

  • One list of those facts
  • “Use only these facts”
  • Saved, so his next question costs about a tenth
The AI never works out a planet’s position. Code does.
2 · Tools · 7 calls in the real run
He asked “when”. So the AI calls tools.
house_reporthis money houses, and who rules them
house_reportthe same, read again from the Moon
find_windowswhen a money period meets a trigger
graha_reporthow strong his money planets are
The AI never guesses a date. Every date comes from a tool.
3 · Checks · for Rahul’s question
Before Rahul sees it, code checks it.
Did it look at both money houses? Code checks the steps.
Did every date come from a tool? Code checks.
Any jargon, like “11th house lord”? Code checks.
Does it promise he’ll be rich? An AI reviewer blocks it.
Code first. Then AI. Then a person. In that order.
4 · Rules
Written down once. Checked by someone else.
The ruleWhere it’s writtenWho checks it
Use only the chart facts. Never work out a date.The expert AI’s instructionsCode. No date tool called? The expert is sent back.
No promises. No diagnosis. Nothing about death.The expert AI’s instructionsAI reviewer. If broken, the answer is rewritten.
No jargon for the person asking.The plain-words AI’s instructionsCode, with a word list. Always rewritten.
Say nothing the expert didn’t say.Built in: the plain-words AI never sees the chartAI reviewer. If broken, the answer is rewritten.
The AI that follows a rule never checks its own work.
Rules in action · Rahul’s real run, shortened
Three steps. Each with limits.
Expert AI · the reader
“Jan 2033 to Jan 2034. Jupiter, his 11th lord, runs as sub-period while Saturn moves through his 2nd house. This is the convergence.”
Plain-words AI · the voice
“The year that stands out most is late January 2033 to early January 2034.”
Never sees his chart
AI reviewer · can only say no
✓ Passed. No flags.
Would reject: “You will be a millionaire by 2033.” That’s a promise.
A writer can make things up. A checker can only say no.
What Rahul got · real run, shortened
Not one date. A sequence.
Jun 2027 – Nov 2028A real step up in base income. A raise, not a windfall.
Jun 2029 – Feb 2031The best time to save. Put money away.
Jan 2033 – Jan 2034The year that stands out most. Many signals line up.
2041 – 2043Later, but possibly the largest of all.
“Money comes through sustained work, not luck.” And no promise of a million.
5 · Memory · for Rahul’s question
An expert spots a gap. It never comes back.
An astrologer reviews it: “You only looked at Jupiter. Start with who rules his money houses.”
That fix becomes a rule for every money question.
And a test, so it is never missed again.
You are not training the model. You are training the harness.
